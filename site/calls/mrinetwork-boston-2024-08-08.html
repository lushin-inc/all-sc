Ellie came off Shad's staffing webinar as a brand-new executive recruiter placing construction executives, and booked the follow-up to get help on two things: candidates inflating their current comp, and not being able to get strong candidates to engage with her. Shad pulled up a whiteboard, drew the behavior-attitude-technique triangle, showed her how to beat the comp question to the punch by giving context before asking, taught the 70/30 rule, and used her best friend as a role-play device to get her off her phone-screen script. He never pitched anything — the call ends with him offering to review her LinkedIn InMails over email for free.

Pure give-first coaching with no sales conversation at all: she agreed to email him sample InMails for feedback, and he warned it might take him a couple of days to reply.

What's missing from a business standpoint: he never asked who runs her office, whether the team buys training, or whether there is a conversation to be had with her managing partner. The one next step is an unpaid InMail review with no date attached.
