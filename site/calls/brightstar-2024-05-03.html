Client call: metrics first, then a $9,500 upsell
Rob LimeStalledProspectTenure at meeting: 0y5m

A recurring coaching call two days after Rob ran an in-person session for the Brightstar team. They debriefed how it landed with Eugene, the veteran rep, and with Bud, the new sales manager, then spent fifteen minutes building the prospecting metrics Bud should hold the team to: stops, people seen, conversations and appointments booked, with the ratios between them used to locate the failure point. Rob then reopened the OMG team assessment that Lauren had previously declined in favor of leadership-only work, named the $9,500 price and the client's own hesitation about it, and offered individual assessments at $500-600 as a smaller entry. Jonathon took it to Lauren with no date attached.

The assessment decision went to Lauren with no deadline; Rob agreed to add Bud to one of the twice-monthly calls and said 'you just let me know and then we'll book it.'
