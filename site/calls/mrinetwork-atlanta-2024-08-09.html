New franchise owner who cannot get clients on the phone
Atlanta; owned by Alejandro; MRI Network franchise
Shad TidlerStalledProspect

Another follow-up to Shad's MRI Network webinar rather than a cold first conversation, so Shad opens with a short agenda and then hands the floor over. Alejandro bought his franchise in January, has placed exactly one person all year and that on a split, and says he can call candidates all day but freezes calling prospective clients, partly because he hears prospects react to his accent. Shad teaches the negative-reverse opener, uses a teenage-nephew analogy to explain why 'here's why we're different' fails, then challenges Alejandro's claim that he has nothing of value to offer a client. He closes with an association-membership strategy, not an offer.

Twenty-seven minutes of free coaching with no qualification, no offer and no next step; Shad ended with 'reach out if there's anything I can help with.'
