Atria Consulting

Four full-desk recruiters, 100 calls, two meetings
Shad TidlerStalledProspect

Emily runs the recruiting team at Atria Consulting, an 18-year-old staffing firm, and was asked last October to pivot herself and then the whole team onto sales. Since April four people have been making roughly 100 cold calls a day and have booked two meetings in three months, revenue is dipping, and she is openly saying she feels like she is failing at training, managing and selling at once. Shad diagnosed it as technique and mindset rather than effort, warned her against scripts, laid out his strategy-structure-staff-skills sequence, and eventually gave a number — roughly $3,000 a month for a team of four plus the evaluation. She said she is comparing three vendors for her CEO and is leaning toward hiring a dedicated senior salesperson instead; he countered with the bird-cage-for-the-bird caution and left it in her court.

No meeting booked — Emily will forward Lushin's website to the CEO as one of three options in a comparison, and promised to let him know either way.
