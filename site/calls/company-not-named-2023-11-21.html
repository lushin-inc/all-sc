Carl heard Rob speak at an executive forum and booked time to continue a side conversation about whether SaaS can sell itself. Rob answers the theory for twenty-five minutes — spectrum of self-serve versus salesperson-led, the deserted-island HubSpot analogy, why 'solution in search of a problem' is usually 'market doesn't care enough' — and makes a few qualifying attempts (what do you sell, who's the ideal client, what problem are you fixing) that Carl absorbs back into theory. When Carl finally says he has no experience building a sales organization, Rob tests timeline instead, gets 'definitely not ready,' and closes with an open invitation.

Carl has no product in market and says he is definitely not ready for a sales organization; Rob ends with 'whenever the time ever strikes you' and sets nothing.

Coaching notes❯

A good study in deciding early whether a conversation is a sale or a favor. Rob spent twenty-five minutes being a thought leader and roughly ninety seconds qualifying; by the time he asked about timeline there was no runway left.
