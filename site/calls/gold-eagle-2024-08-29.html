HVAC founder rates himself a one as a sales leader
residential/commercial HVAC
Rob LimeAdvancedProspect

Keating started an HVAC company two years out of high school and now runs about $3M with three technicians, two install crews, an outside and an inside salesperson. Olga, a family friend, watched his team in her own home and told him his technicians are strong technically and weak in front of customers — which is why he took the call. Rob moved him off the people problem and onto numbers (memberships converting at 18% against a 50% target, reviews at 7%, average ticket below industry), then onto his own sales leadership, where Keating rated himself a one out of ten on coaching, accountability and motivation. Rob reframed the engagement from sales training to structure and leadership development, laid out the strategy-structure-staff-skills pyramid, and booked a follow-up with a pre-work survey.

Booked Wednesday at 10:00 Central for sixty minutes, with a six-minute survey sent the same day; Rob said they would pick up exactly where they left off.
