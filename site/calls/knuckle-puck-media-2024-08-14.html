Knuckle Puck Media

Split-role VP tests whether sales is really her job
SEO and PPC agency
Rob LimeAdvancedProspect

Meghan booked the call off a referral from outsourced CFO Paul Seaton and arrives running two jobs: operations plus a brand-new business development role she took in April. Her quarterly target is 350k to proposal stage and she has produced 20k; all real selling still runs through CEO Brett, and Knuckle Puck's one prior BD hire delivered ten percent of a million dollar goal in 2022. Rob tells her plainly that he hates split sales roles, runs a commitment test on whether she will do the unglamorous ninety percent, and converts the whole conversation into a sales assessment plus a review meeting booked for Friday.

She agreed to take the 45-minute assessment that afternoon, and they booked a 60-minute review for Friday 2:00 to 3:00 Eastern.
