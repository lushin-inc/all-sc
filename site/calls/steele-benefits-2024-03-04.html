Coaching notes❯

A textbook short FTC: up-front contract inside the first minute, four pain questions, a permission-asked prescription, an up-front contract for the next meeting, and a booked in-person hour — all in 23 minutes.

'Why 400%?' is the move to steal. Two words against a number the prospect volunteered, and it produces his real decision criteria (organic growth, no debt, protect the culture) for free.

Asking 'can I make a suggestion here?' before prescribing is cheap and effective — it converts a pitch into an invited recommendation and the prospect accepted the next step without negotiating.

The weak stretch is 16:35–19:36, where Shad hands back a long four-area framework. It is accurate but it runs nearly three minutes, and it cost him the chance to dig into the brother, the retiring door-opener, or the under-built pipeline — all of which were sitting open.
