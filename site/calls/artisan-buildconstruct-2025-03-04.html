Owner-only rainmaker buys a year of sales infrastructure
Rob LimeAdvancedProspect

Tony owns a design-build remodeling and construction firm, personally sells every job, and wants out of being the only rainmaker — a salesperson hired, a system built, and eventually a manager running it. Rob Lime opens by putting scope and money on the table before any detail: $42,000 for twelve months, immediately reframed as $3,500 a month and handed back as an ROI test. He then spends the rest of the call answering Tony's real objections — the time commitment, whether Rob understands a complex sales cycle, and whether the infrastructure Tony already has gets thrown away. Tony commits verbally at 13:26, pushes hard on hiring philosophy and contract terms, and the call ends with an MOU to send and a working session booked for Monday the 17th at 9:00 Central.

Tony committed verbally to $3,500 a month, asked for the MOU and payment terms, and booked Monday the 17th at 9:00 Central for the first working session.
