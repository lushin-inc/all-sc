VP tests her team's OMG data, then asks pricing
Aaron PrickelAdvancedProspectTenure at meeting: 1y6m

Aaron ran OMG assessments across Fischer Homes' sales counselors — tops, middles, bottoms, plus two people already let go — and this hour is Emily, their VP of Sales, testing the data against people she knows cold. She arrives half-skeptical and uses her hardest cases to try to break the instrument: Brenda scored a 95 sales percentile and was fired, Katie is an elite coach with low commitment. Aaron answers each by narrowing what OMG claims to measure rather than defending it, then shows her the three kick-out triggers and benchmarks the whole team against 112,000 assessments. By the end she is asking whether she can run the current team through it for coaching, whether there is a management version for succession planning, and for pricing.

Emily asked for pricing in writing, a sample management assessment, and explicit permission to be chased; Aaron committed to send it the same day with his calendar link embedded so the next meeting books itself.
