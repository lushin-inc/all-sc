Case IH / New Holland
Aaron PrickelAdvancedProspectTenure at meeting: Pre

This is the earlier of the two CNH calls. Aaron and Rob walk Morgan through an initial-draft two-day selling-skills program for CNH's dealer network, built from what Morgan and James had told them, and after every section ask whether they are drastically off. Morgan — who runs CNH's commercial training and is the only buyer present — supplies the nuances that matter: dealer salesperson turnover since the pandemic, keeping customers "red and blue" against Deere and Kubota rather than only stealing share, and a mid-range product line so cluttered that seven tractors share a PTO rating. He then volunteers the whole timeline backwards from CNH's December training catalog, admits he is running one competing vendor pilot, and says Lushin were the only vendor who offered to tailor anything.

Morgan will review it with James and come back with pilot attendees and tentative dates; a 15-minute check-in was booked for Tuesday, February 17th at 4:00 Eastern, with the deck and recording promised that night.
