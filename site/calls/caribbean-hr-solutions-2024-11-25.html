Caribbean HR Solutions

Referral exec sizes up OMG for a recruiting firm
Rob LimeAdvancedProspect

Damion was handed off by his colleague Deborah after Caribbean HR Solutions was asked by one of Rob's Indiana clients to assess every offshore SDR candidate before interview, and Deborah wanted a second opinion on the tool. Damion joined two minutes late, asked to be treated as a novice, and Rob spent most of the call on one idea — that background and experience do not predict selling capability — rather than on features. Rob then tested what Deborah had actually asked for before agreeing to anything, and proposed a three-way meeting instead of a demo. Damion closed by offering to run two live candidates through the assessment as a test.

Damion said he would reply to Rob's email, loop in Deborah, and lock a three-way meeting the following week, and asked to put two pending candidates through the tool as a trial.

Coaching notes❯

There is no up-front contract anywhere in this call. Damion joined late and Rob went straight to explaining; no time frame, no agenda, no exit ramp. It worked only because Damion asked for the story himself.

The single best move is 'What was Deborah's outlook?' — one question that did the work of an entire decision-process block and saved Rob from pitching the wrong person.

Rob disqualified parts of OMG out loud ('here's where it actually doesn't help'), which bought more trust than any feature list would have.

The opportunity is still completely unsized going into the three-way: no hire volume, no cost of a bad hire, no money of any kind was discussed.
