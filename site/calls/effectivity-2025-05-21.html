Solo founder with no pipeline asks whether to quit
Brian KavickyUnclearC-Level1on1

Annette runs a one-person consultancy cold-calling executive search firms. She has just come off four weeks of flu, has nobody in her pipeline, and has exhausted her original 60-name list. Brian spends the first half as a tooling consult — stop researching prospects before calls, get to 14 touches before you give up on a name, buy Apollo rather than a CRM — and the second half on what she actually called about, which is whether to shut the business. He refuses the "tell me I don't have it" framing, reframes entrepreneurship as a bet on time rather than talent, and tells her to solve the income problem separately so the business gets more runway. She leaves committed to buying Apollo and building a 300-name call list.

A coaching call with an existing client, not a sale. It ends with Annette promising to pursue Apollo and have a 300-name list by the next conversation; no date is set.
