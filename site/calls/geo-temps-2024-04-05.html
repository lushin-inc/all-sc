Coach disqualifies a staffing deal as an ops problem
Shad TidlerNo fitProspect

This is the joint meeting Taralu booked at the end of her March 27 first conversation, with her operations manager Doug Oster in the room. Doug laid out the picture: roughly 130 customer agreements and only 17 active, direct hire underperforming, the temp side needing to go from about 100 people to 160 just to cover overhead, and a reputation damaged by predecessors who stopped calling clients. Shad dug into why the clients left, asked what the customers themselves would need to see, and exposed that Doug's ideal customer profile and Taralu's hunting were not aligned. Then he asked permission to be candid and told them the problem was operations and fulfillment rather than sales, coached them for free on reopening the lapsed accounts by phone, and left without proposing anything.

Shad told them he was not sure where he could help, gave them a reopening script and a farm-your-17-clients plan, and closed with an open invitation rather than a next meeting.
