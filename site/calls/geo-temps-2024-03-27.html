Geo Temps

Account manager handed a hunting role she's never done
Shad TidlerAdvancedProspect

Taralu has spent 27 years in staffing on the account-management side and was just asked to take over business development at Geo Temps, an eight-person mining-niche staffing firm that is not profitable. Her quota is two to three new clients billing per quarter; she has been reactivating lapsed accounts and has sent 19 renewal contracts, but has never cold called and has no plan, no ideal client profile and no goals of her own. Shad asked permission to keep digging, separated the company's goal from hers, surfaced that the previous BD rep did nothing for two years without anyone noticing, then walked through Lushin's strategy-structure-staff-skills model and deferred all cost and time questions to a meeting with her manager. He booked that joint call on the spot.

A one-hour joint meeting with her operations manager Doug Oster was scheduled for the following Friday, with an up-front contract for what that meeting decides.
