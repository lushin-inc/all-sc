advising Neody
Brian KavickyAdvancedC-Level1on1

Robert's advisory firm has been inside Neody for about eighteen months and he has now taken the role of go-between for Derek, the inventor-CEO, and a four-person sales team that is throwing things at the wall. Lushin already trained one of those reps, Charles, and Brian personally interpreted the assessments on two of the others and gave hiring and management recommendations that were accepted and then ignored. Rather than re-pitch, Brian went back to that history, asked what changed, and then asked the only question that mattered — who is going to require this to happen, and is that you. Robert claimed the sponsor role and the call ended with Brian agreeing to run a diagnostic clinic on one of Neody's Monday sales meetings.

Brian agreed to send dates and times to run a clinic-style session on a Monday 11am sales meeting; Robert pulled the date forward from the 27th to as soon as next week, and the broader referral-partner idea was discussed but left without a next step.

Coaching notes❯

This is a re-entry, not a cold first conversation. Lushin already trained Charles and Brian personally assessed two of the four reps. His best move is refusing to treat the meeting as a fresh start — 'I want to go back in time a little bit' resets the whole frame.

'Who is going to reinforce this? Is that you?' is the load-bearing question. Brian establishes a sponsor with firing authority before he will spend an hour on their sales meeting.

Nothing was priced and nothing was asked about budget, even though Robert opened the money door twice ('I know what Neody's paying you,' 'what it would look like to do what we're thinking about'). The clinic is free work with no agreed decision attached to it — the ufc-next says 'we'll decide after that' but never says who decides what.

The strategic-partner idea is the bigger opportunity in the call — Robert has 30,000 LinkedIn connections and says 95% of the CEOs he works with name sales as the problem — and it got a good philosophical answer but no next step of its own.
