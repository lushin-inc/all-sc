Coaching notes❯

The buyer ran the qualification here, not the coach — competitors, how he knows Mark, sales-only versus EOS, company size. Shad answered all four well, but a coach should notice he never asked the reciprocal question: what would make this session a success for your members, or what do you need them to walk away able to do?

No up-front contract. The call opens into small talk and never gets a frame, which is part of why roughly six of nineteen minutes go to an uninterrupted walkthrough of the four S's.

Best steal is the 1:16 career question. Asking what came before, rather than what do you do, got a former utility CEO talking for ninety seconds and warmed the whole call.

He recovered the close well: 'when's your next meeting and when would be good to touch base' turned a vague I'll-call-you into a held January 29 slot and a defined check-in after October 15.
