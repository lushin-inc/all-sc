Public Media Apps

The yes arrives in sixty seconds, then logistics
Aaron PrickelClosedProspectTenure at meeting: Pre

The third and final call in the Public Media Apps arc, one week after the OMG debrief and the $1,950 number. Aaron opens with banter, turns the floor over at 1:01, and Peter leads with 'we'd like to move forward' plus a request to push the start to mid-February. Aaron accepts the delay, extends it further rather than defending the date, and converts the rest of the nine minutes into sequencing: a February 20 three-to-five block found live on screen, HubSpot dashboard and pipeline-definition homework, and a plan to give Kate a month's written notice before she is pulled in. No re-selling, no celebration.

Peter opened with 'we'd like to move forward,' pushed the start to the week of February 17, and the call became pure logistics — February 20 structure session booked, pre-work requested, Kate joining in March.

Coaching notes❯

Call three of three, one week after the OMG debrief and the $1,950. Aaron runs a yes/no by not running one: he turns the floor over at 1:01, stays silent, and lets Peter announce the decision. Worth studying as the alternative to asking 'so, what did you decide?'

When the yes came with a delay, Aaron extended the delay further instead of defending the start date. Agreeing with a postponement you can afford is cheaper than arguing with a buyer who just said yes.

Within ninety seconds the call is pure sequencing — a date found live on screen, a short homework list, and who gets looped in when. Nothing is re-sold and the price is never mentioned again.

The Kate handling is the most transferable thing here: a month's notice by email so the rep being coached is never ambushed into a training schedule.
