Ovyl

Owner buying out his partner inherits the sales seat
Nashville product development / hardware engineering studio, ovyl.io
Rob LimeAdvancedProspect

Evan is partway through buying out the partner who has been doing all of Ovyl's selling, financed by a personal loan at nearly 10% with roughly $16k monthly payments. He called because sales is the survival risk and the first question everyone will ask. Rob worked the numbers: 2024 is tracking to $3.2M against a $4.5M plan, two large projects short, with a loss last quarter; lead flow is a new marketing agency, a LinkedIn outreach vendor, chip-manufacturer partnerships and, by Evan's own account, dumb luck. Rob challenged the idea that long client timelines are untouchable, asked what ROI would make a $30,000 year worth it, and then sequenced the Sales X-Ray assessment ahead of the infrastructure work Evan wanted to start with.

Booked Wednesday at noon for 75 minutes to complete the assessment, begin on infrastructure, and talk scope and terms; the invite went out during the call and the assessment link was promised same day or next.
