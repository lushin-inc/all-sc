Logistics call on an already-sold association training program
member/partner trade association
Shad TidlerClosedProspect

This is a delivery-planning call on a sold engagement, not a first conversation. Doug walks Shad through the two-part program OMA is rolling out: two breakout sessions at the annual meeting in the Phoenix area open to all salespeople, managers and owners, followed by monthly virtual sessions OMA will charge a small fee for, plus a tailored cohort of eight partners chosen because they each do between half a million and a million dollars of business with other OMA partners. Most of the call is schedule negotiation around Shad's flights, solved when Doug offers the Monday morning before the meeting starts. It ends with Doug telling Shad to invoice for the annual meeting work and Shad promising a written agreement for the select group.

Doug approved the pricing as sent and told Shad to invoice for the annual meeting; Shad committed to formalizing the select-group piece in writing for signature.
