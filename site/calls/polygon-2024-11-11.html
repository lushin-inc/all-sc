Not a first conversation — this is an OMG sales managerial assessment debrief with an existing client. Alex is the interim sales manager and a candidate to take the VP of Sales job permanently; Bree is still running an external search. Rob gives the verdict up front (he should expect to succeed if he wants the role), teaches how to read an assessment, explains why will outranks DNA outranks tactical, and walks the coaching, motivating and buy cycle findings. Alex asks the honest question — is hiring me a risk? — and gets an honest answer. Bree stays on afterward for a private debrief where she says her instinct is still no, and the conversation turns to whether Polygon needs both a VP and a manager, and to 2025 commission structure.

The verdict before the data
“From the assessment's perspective, you should expect to succeed in that role if you wanted to. That's the verdict from the assessment.”
Leads with the answer so the subject can hear the rest of the findings without waiting for a judgment. A model opening for any debrief.

Three buckets for reading your own results
“There'll be findings you agree with, findings you understand but might not agree with, and findings you might not agree with because you don't get the context yet. A difference in definition oftentimes accounts for the gap.”

The buy cycle we actually want
