Despite the filename, this is the second of the two CNH calls in this set — Morgan had taken the draft program to James and came back to say it is on the right path. Aaron spends the whole eighteen minutes converting that agreement into specifics: which month, which two days, which building, how many people in the room, and exactly what James needs in a quote to cut a purchase order. Two Lushin coaches are on: Aaron (Speaker 3) sells, Rob Lime (Speaker 1) plays the room. Worth watching as an example of a second coach adding warmth without diluting the ask.

He never re-sells the program. Eighteen minutes goes entirely to specificity — month, days, building, headcount, quote format, PO path — because the decision was already made. Compare to how long a coach would normally spend re-presenting at this stage.

Price out loud before being asked, then immediate permission to shop. The combination is cheap when the real competitor is inaction, and three weeks earlier Morgan had already named tailoring, not price, as the deciding factor.
