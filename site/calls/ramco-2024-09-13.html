Coaching a loyal customer's quiet price defection
metal roofing and panel supply
Rob LimeUnclearProspect

Stan brings Rob a scenario he raised in a group session: Arlen, a contractor Stan has a long personal history with and gives extraordinary service to, quietly bought an $18,000 order from a cheaper competitor — and when Stan drilled in, it turned out to be the third order in a month. Rob narrows it to a binary (Arlen has either lost awareness of what Ramco does for him or he doesn't care), teaches commitment and consistency as the tool for changing future behavior, and role plays the confrontation. The call lands on Stan's real insight — that Arlen doesn't know how often his own foremen lean on Ramco after hours — and Rob's correction that Stan told him instead of asking him.

Not a sales call — a coaching session that ended with Rob asking Stan to state his takeaway and how he would use it, which Stan did.
