Consulting CEO buys assessments for two engineer partners
Aaron PrickelAdvancedProspectTenure at meeting: Pre

Austin runs a three-year-old consulting firm that has tripled annually on referrals alone, with him personally handling 80-90% of client relationships and new business. His two engineer partners are moving from doing the work to owning relationships and have no sales framework, and he has decided he is not the right person to teach them. Aaron let him talk himself into the problem, then asked whether it was worth $1,500 to get the data before describing a single thing Lushin does. Call ended with three OMG evaluations agreed, an in-person debrief set for late July, and the retainer range on the table.

Austin agreed to all three completing the evaluation, gave Aaron his partners' names and spellings, and they set an in-person 90-minute debrief for the last week of July with Dana coordinating.
