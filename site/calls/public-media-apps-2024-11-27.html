Former trainee calls his old trainer about dad's company
Aaron PrickelAdvancedProspectTenure at meeting: Pre

Colin sat in on Lushin's training four years earlier as an inbound SDR at OnBoard, left after a PE takeover, and joined his father Pete's eight-person company to build its sales and marketing function from nothing. He laid out the real problem: Kate is effectively an SDR, Pete does every 60-minute feature-dump demo himself while managing everyone, and they lost roughly 30 deals last year with no real objection handling or follow-up process. Aaron asked for outcomes first, used four or five diagnostic questions to separate the leadership problem from the skill problem, and sized the opportunity out loud before agreeing to go further. The call ended with Dana booking a 60-minute three-way with Pete and homework to be sent beforehand.

Aaron handed scheduling to Dana for a 60-minute call with Colin and Pete after Thanksgiving, and committed to sending both of them pre-work that would drive that conversation.
