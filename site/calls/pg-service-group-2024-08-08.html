PG Service Group

Owner hires a coach before the problems start
an exterior services / valet trash company in Central Florida, held by Carlos's investment fund
Shad TidlerAdvancedProspect

Carlos owns an investment fund and a two-and-a-half-year-old exterior services company, PG Service Group, that sells valet trash into Central Florida resorts and short-term rental communities on two-to-five-year contracts. He has just moved Jennifer — likable, industry-experienced, no formal sales training — into business development three weeks ago, and he wants the coaching in place before she struggles rather than after. Alejandra, who will manage her, admitted she has no framework for setting goals or holding anyone accountable. Shad walked them through strategy, structure, staff and skills, declined to price anything, and sent a four-S survey as homework ahead of a deeper conversation booked on the call for the following Thursday.

Next Thursday at 2:00 p.m. Eastern booked and the invite sent before they hung up, with a ten-minute four-S survey to be completed separately by Carlos and Alejandra beforehand.

Coaching notes❯

A pre-emptive engagement rather than a rescue: the buyer has installed a brand-new rep and wants the coaching in place before she fails. Notice that Shad still runs the same sequence — problem, why, impact, structure — and finds the real gap in Alejandra, not Jennifer.

He refuses to price twice (16:15 and 22:41) and holds it, unlike the Atria call the same month. The homework survey is what lets him hold it: he has a concrete reason the number does not exist yet.

Best practice on the close: he opens the calendar on the call, offers a window, books it, and sends both the invite and the homework before hanging up, with the agenda and the no-harm-no-foul exit stated for the next meeting.

What's thin: he never asked who signs or what Carlos would allocate, and he did not probe the two-year sales cycle against the three-year market window — a one-rep plan may not reach $5M in that time, which is the strategic question underneath the whole engagement.
