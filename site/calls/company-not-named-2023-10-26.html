Rob LimeAdvancedProspect

Robert joined a VR and AR custom training-simulation company in June carrying a two-million-dollar quota, and found no ideal client profile, no case studies, no testimonials, an out-of-date website and a parent-company marketing team that has produced one trade show booth in two years. Rob positioned Lushin's experience in highly technical sales where the market doesn't yet know how to buy, told Robert he can't hang his quota on a marketing department that hasn't delivered, and established that Matt would come in only after Robert was convinced. He corrected Robert's assumption that the next call would be another fifteen minutes and booked forty-five.

Booked a 45-minute Zoom for Thursday at 8:45 Mountain, with Robert asking that it be honed in on what Lushin can do specifically for a high-tech company in his situation.
