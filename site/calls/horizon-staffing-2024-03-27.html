Four people from Horizon Staffing bring Shad live sales questions from a webinar follow-up: what to say when a prospect already uses five staffing firms, how to get past gatekeepers, and what voicemail actually gets a call back. He answers all three with scripts — a negative reverse on the five-vendor brush-off, the buyer's-process explanation of why people hang up, cadence and twelve-to-fifteen attempts, and the 'I don't even know if you're the right person, can you help me out' callback. At 27:23 he flips to selling, asks what else they're running into, gets soft answers, and uses his own reversal on them — which Paula catches and calls out.

Lisa agrees to Shad emailing to set time for a deeper conversation about where they are and where they want to get to, and he agrees to send the recording for the team.
