Julie and Eric attended Shad's webinars and booked time to ask two questions: how to get contact information for decision makers, and how to unstick proposals that go into limbo. Grant, the outside salesperson the call was partly about, never joined. Shad split the call cleanly in two, recommended ZoomInfo, RocketReach and Lusha, argued for the phone over email, pushed back on their company's goody-bag doctrine, taught the 30-second commercial structure and his voicemail scripts, then reframed every stalled proposal as a priority problem rather than a price problem. He closed with a soft probe about whether the rest of Exact Staff has the same issues, offered to send the recording, and booked nothing.

Thirty minutes of coaching delivered with no up-front contract, no qualification of the account and no next meeting; he offered the recording link and said 'reach back out if I can help.'
