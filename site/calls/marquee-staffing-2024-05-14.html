Marquee Staffing

COO inherits a sales team with no system
Shad TidlerAdvancedProspect

Renee was promoted from finance to COO of a ~$30M staffing firm and discovered there was no sales program at all — no KPIs, no process, no objections handling, and a semi-retired CEO who had been the de facto rainmaker. She has hired three salespeople, implemented HubSpot, and is now personally playing bad cop because the senior exec who should manage the team is 'too nice.' Shad refused to diagnose for her, pushing 'why do you think' repeatedly until she concluded it was technique and mindset rather than knowledge, then laid out the evaluation ($4,500) and coaching ($600/person/month) and asked directly whether those were scary numbers. The call ended with a scheduled follow-up and two industry referrals to be sent by Tuesday.

Renee asked for staffing-industry referrals and a written overview; they booked a decision conversation for the 24th at 5:30 Eastern / 2:30 Pacific before hanging up.

Coaching notes❯

Watch the question discipline: across the first 25 minutes he asks 'why do you think' five separate times and never once supplies the answer. Every useful fact in this call came out of her mouth.

Money is handled flatly and in sequence — $300 per person, $1,500, $3,000 overview, $4,500 total, then $600 per person per month — with no softening and an immediate 'are those scary numbers doable.' No discount, no flinch.

He never pinned down who actually signs. She says 'we're fine financially' and talks about the CEO as a separate voice, but he never asks whether the CEO has to approve a $40K-plus commitment.

Minutes 45-56 drift into answering her questions about HubSpot, cold calling vs marketing, and his ASA history — good credibility, but it is presenting, and it pushed the close into the last five minutes of a 63-minute call.
