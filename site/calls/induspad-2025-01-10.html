Owner wants ten million without owning the accountability
EXO warehousing
Rob LimeAdvancedProspect

Jitender is trying to take EXO from roughly $3.5M to $10M across two new warehouse locations and needs to add salespeople to do it. Rob made him score his existing team one to ten, landed on an average of 1.5 out of 10, then spent ten minutes on pallet economics before accepting the $10M target as real. The heart of the call is Rob telling him plainly that he can supply system and structure but cannot supply the commitment and accountability, which has to come from an equity holder — and Jitender conceding he is not yet in a position to remove himself from sales. Jitender then declined the $3,000 Chicago strategy session and counter-proposed hiring help first; Rob took the pivot and put a Thursday 4pm invite out on the call.

Jitender redirected the engagement from a paid strategy session to 'first help me hire the sales people'; Rob agreed, deferred the cost question to the next conversation, and sent the Thursday 4pm invite before hanging up.

The one-to-ten rep scoring is the cheapest diagnostic in the batch — it produced a 1.5 average in four minutes and set up the best question in the call ('would you attract more of the same?').

The block from 30:02 to 39:40 is the model for an owner-accountability conversation: say what you cannot do, name where commitment has to come from, use yourself as the example, grant the long-term goal, then pre-frame the exact future moment you will need them to reinforce you.
