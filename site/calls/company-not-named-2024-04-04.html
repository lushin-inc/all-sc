Murray runs a 32-person data-collection firm that did $4M last year and is tracking to $5M, but the new Florida office is stuck: roughly $250-300K last year, $150K so far this year, against a target over half a million. The rep there follows up poorly and nobody has been clearly assigned to hold him accountable. Rob diagnosed the rep, the accountability gap and the reactive goal-setting, then stopped and told Murray directly that 'we could get better' people rarely change anything, which pulled out the real stake: they cannot float Florida's cost forever. He proposed OMG assessments on Murray, Nate in Georgia and the Florida rep at $1,200, quoted roughly $3,000 a month for ongoing work, and asked to be in the decision conversation.

Murray took it to his leadership meeting the following week; Rob agreed to send a Vidyard walkthrough instead of being in the room, and left the next call to Murray booking from his calendar link.
