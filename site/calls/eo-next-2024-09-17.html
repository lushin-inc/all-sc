EO Next

Rob on the buying side of a sponsorship pitch
EO Next (national Entrepreneurs' Organization chapter) — the selling party; the buyer here is Lushin
Rob LimeStalledProspect

The roles are reversed on this one: Matt, a newly seated board member of the EO Next chapter, is pitching Rob on an exclusive $20,000-a-year sales coaching sponsorship, and Rob is the prospect. Rob opens by handing Matt all the reasons Lushin wouldn't do it, then runs his own qualification — price, retreat costs, access versus true membership, chapter size, the one member who actually wants a sales coach — while narrating out loud how he would cash flow it against an ESOP note that has their net margin at one percent. It ends with Matt sending a brochure and offering a reference call, and no date on the calendar.

No commitment and no scheduled next meeting — Matt will send a brochure and possibly connect Rob with another sponsor, and Rob will 'let him know' his interest.
