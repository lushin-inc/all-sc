MRI Network member firm, Manchester, UK

This is a follow-up to an MRI Network webinar Shad ran two weeks earlier, not a cold first conversation, so Sandra opens by reporting what she already changed because of it rather than being qualified. Her stated problem is that clients will not make decisions: a critical quality-director search has stalled because the acting CEO and FD will not take her call, and three live proposals are sitting in three-way bake-offs. Shad whiteboards behavior/attitude/technique, scripts a blunt two-choices email, and pushes on why she never asks prospects why they are dragging it out. When he finally asks whether she wants help, she redirects into inviting him to speak to MRI's international community instead.

No commercial next step with Sandra; she agreed to email-introduce Shad to Sam Collins so he could speak to MRI's international group.
